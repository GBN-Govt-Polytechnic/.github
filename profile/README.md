## About

Guru BrahmaNand Ji Government Polytechnic, Nilokheri is one of the oldest technical institutions in North India. Established by the Government of India in 1947, it began as the Govt. School of Engineering at Rasool (West Punjab) and moved to Nilokheri after Partition.

Today the institute serves 600+ diploma students across seven programmes on a 26-acre campus on GT Road. This GitHub organization is the official home of the software that supports the institute.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Branches** | Computer · Civil · Electrical · Electronics & Communication · Mechanical · Instrumentation & Control · Mechatronics |
| **Library** | 100,000+ volumes with a book bank facility |
| **Campus life** | NCC, Poly-Fest, Annual Athletic Meet, Training & Placement Cell |
