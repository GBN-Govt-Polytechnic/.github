## About the institute

GBN Govt. Polytechnic is one of the premier technical institutions in the country. It was the first polytechnic to be set up in North India, began as the Govt. School of Engineering in Rasool (West Punjab), and moved to Nilokheri after partition.

- **Motto**: Provide Technical Education to All
- **Established**: 1947 by the Govt. of India, re-established at Nilokheri in 1951
- **Recognition**: Approved by AICTE, New Delhi · Affiliated to HSBTE, Panchkula
- **Campus**: 26 acres on GT Road (NH-1), 143 km from Delhi
- **Students**: 600+ diploma students across seven programmes
- **Branches**: Computer · Civil · Electrical · Electronics & Communication · Mechanical · Instrumentation & Control · Mechatronics
- **Library**: 100,000+ volumes with a book bank
- **Campus life**: NCC since 1960, Poly-Fest, annual Athletic Meet, Training & Placement Cell
